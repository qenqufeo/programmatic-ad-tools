## FAQ

**How do programmatic advertising platforms differ from direct publisher relationships?**

Programmatic platforms aggregate inventory from multiple publishers through SSPs and exchanges, letting you access thousands of sites through one dashboard with automated bidding and targeting. Direct publisher relationships require individual negotiations, insertion orders, and manual campaign management per site, which scales poorly beyond a handful of properties. The automation and inventory breadth of programmatic platforms enable testing hundreds of placements quickly while optimizing toward winners, whereas direct buys work better when you've identified specific premium placements worth premium rates and guaranteed inventory.

**What's the real difference between push, pop, and native traffic beyond just ad formats?**

The formats reflect different user intent states. Native traffic catches users actively consuming content, making them receptive to related recommendations that feel editorial. Push notifications reach opted-in audiences who previously engaged with sites or apps, bringing some existing relationship context. Pop traffic interrupts browsing with forced exposure, converting through volume and aggressive offers rather than content alignment, working best for direct response offers with immediate value propositions rather than consideration purchases. Your offer determines which format matches the buying mindset—push for utilities and apps, native for content-driven products, pops for aggressive sweepstakes and gambling.

**Should I start with premium networks like Taboola or cheaper alternatives when testing new offers?**

Start with mid-tier networks like MGID, PropellerAds, or RichAds that balance reasonable traffic quality with affordable testing costs. Premium networks like Taboola and Outbrain charge 2-3x higher CPCs that can kill profitability during testing phases when conversion rates haven't been optimized yet. Once you've validated an offer converts profitably on cheaper traffic and optimized landing pages and funnels, scale to premium networks where higher-intent traffic justifies the costs. Testing on expensive inventory before proving product-market fit burns budgets unnecessarily—validate cheaply, then upgrade to quality.
